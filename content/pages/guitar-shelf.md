# The Guitar Shelf

Every color on this site was stolen from a guitar. This page is the confession.

## The finishes

In the 1950s, Fender borrowed its custom colors straight from car paint catalogs — **Surf Green** from a 1957 Chevrolet, **Candy Apple Red** sprayed metallic over a gold base coat, **Lake Placid Blue** from a Cadillac. Sunburst came from somewhere older: the lacquered violin tradition, amber at the heart fading to burnt edges.

Those five finishes became this site's five themes. The gold sparkle you see inside the red particle swarm isn't decoration — real candy apple is translucent red over gold, and the swarm respects the recipe.

## The instruments

- An **offset in surf green** — jangle, rosewood, the reason the home page is calm
- A **workhorse with humbuckers** — for the days that need weight
- A **dreadnought in three-tone sunburst** — porch-certified, blog-section-certified
