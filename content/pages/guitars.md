# Guitars

The analog stack: latency zero, uptime whenever I feel like it.

## Why guitars, on an engineering site

Because they're the same discipline wearing different clothes. A signal chain is a pipeline. A pedalboard is a microservice fleet with better knobs. Tone-chasing is performance tuning where the benchmark is your own ear — subjective, unrepeatable, and completely worth it. Engineering all day teaches you to remove surprises; playing at night reminds you why you liked them.

## The instruments

An **offset in surf green** with a rosewood board for jangle. A **humbucker workhorse** for days that need weight. A **dreadnought in three-tone sunburst** that has never once been deployed indoors when a porch was available.

## The chain

Tube amp held at the edge of breakup — the analog equivalent of running a system at the interesting part of its load curve. A pedalboard whose event log only appends; nothing is ever truly removed, merely unpublished to a drawer.

## The colors

Surf Green, Candy Apple Red, Lake Placid Blue, three-tone Sunburst — Fender borrowed them from 1950s car paint, and this site borrowed them from Fender. The full confession, finish by finish, is on [the guitar shelf](#/page/guitar-shelf).
