# Rolling Garage

[rollinggarage.cl](https://rollinggarage.cl) belongs to a good friend who fixes cars — not in a workshop you drive to, but at your home or office, anywhere in the **Región del Biobío**.

Not every project needs an event log and a control panel. This one needed to be **elegant, decent, and fast**, and to answer one question in the first three seconds: *can this person come fix my car, and are they serious?* Dark hero, hands on an engine, the promise stated plainly in Spanish, one obvious call to action, services below.

## What the brief really was

A mobile mechanic sells trust before they sell labour. The site's whole job is to look like someone who will show up on time and know what they're doing. So: restrained typography, real photography instead of stock cheer, no carousel, no popups, no cookie theatre — and quick on a phone signal in a parking lot, because that is where it gets read.

## Why it's here

Because a portfolio that only shows distributed systems is a portfolio that quietly says "I don't do small work." Some of the most satisfying projects are the ones where a friend's business gets a front door it deserves.
