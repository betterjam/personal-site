# This Site

The most honest portfolio piece is the one you're standing in. This site is a working demonstration of everything the other sections claim to believe.

## The claims, made structural

- **The log is the truth**: every post and page — including this one — is a projection over an append-only event log in PostgreSQL. Restart the server and the content reassembles itself from event one.
- **Deploys are events too**: the banner on the home page reads the deploy history back from the same log, commit hashes and all.
- **Content exists once**: sections, cards, and pages reference the same atoms. Editing a page in the maintainer console reshapes its card everywhere it appears.

## The craft parts

A GSAP frontend with no framework: sections on a snapped scroll track, a particle swarm that recolors mid-flight as it carries you between finishes, and a maintainer console styled like the dev tool it is. The [colophon](#/page/colophon) has the full credits, including the AI pair who committed most of the riffs.

## What it's teaching me

Building in the open, with the under-construction banner as a feature instead of an apology, turns "not finished" into "watch this space." Which, to be fair, is also how the [pedalboard](#/page/guitars) works.
